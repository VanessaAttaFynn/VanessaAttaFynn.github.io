02 About

The journey so far

I'm a Systems Developer at Attain Enterprise Solutions, currently working with Ghana's National Pensions Regulatory Authority, and have just completed an MSc in Computer Science at the University of Ghana.

My path through computer science has been anything but a straight line, and I think that's what I've come to love most about it. From exploring the possibilities of machine learning to building enterprise systems and finding my place in research, each experience has opened a door I hadn't quite imagined before. Somewhere along the way, I've learned to embrace unfamiliar challenges, trust my curiosity, and find joy in seeing ideas grow into something real.
